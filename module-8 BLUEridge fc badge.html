Borussia Dortmund FC

The hardest shape to position was the large “V” in the center of the badge because I had to make sure it was centered between the two B letters while keeping the entire logo balanced inside the circular border. I used the SVG x and y coordinates to move the letters until they lined up correctly. The first color combination I tried did not work because I used lighter colors that did not create enough contrast between the badge and the lettering. I changed the design to a strong black-and-yellow combination, which made the letters much easier to see and gave the badge a stronger appearance. Outside of class, I have seen similar SVG-style badges and logos on sports team websites, sports merchandise, and digital graphics. Many team logos use simple geometric shapes, bold lettering, and strong colors because SVG allows them to stay sharp when displayed at different sizes. Creating this badge helped me understand how SVG coordinates, text, shapes, and color can work together to create a recognizable design.
